# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Title

**STEMQuest: Gamified Learning Platform for Rural Students**

## Problem Statement

Many students in rural areas develop fear and disinterest in STEM subjects (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) due to:

* Difficult concepts and lack of practical exposure
* Traditional teaching methods
* Limited access to quality learning resources
* Fear of examinations and failure
* Lack of motivation and engagement

As a result, students often avoid STEM careers and perform poorly in these subjects.

---

## Vision

To transform STEM education into an engaging adventure where rural students learn through games, challenges, rewards, and real-world activities, helping them overcome fear and build confidence.

---

## Objective

Develop a gamified learning platform that:

* Makes STEM subjects fun and interactive
* Reduces study anxiety
* Improves concept understanding
* Encourages self-learning
* Increases STEM participation among rural students

---

## Target Users

### Primary Users

* Students from Class 6–12
* Rural and semi-rural schools

### Secondary Users

* Teachers
* Parents
* School administrators

---

## Key Features

### 1. Learning Quests

Students complete topic-based missions.

Example:

* Fractions Adventure
* Electricity Explorer
* Coding Ninja
* Space Scientist Challenge

Reward:

* XP Points
* Badges
* Coins

---

### 2. Level-Based Progression

| Level   | Status            |
| ------- | ----------------- |
| Level 1 | Beginner Explorer |
| Level 2 | STEM Learner      |
| Level 3 | Problem Solver    |
| Level 4 | Innovator         |
| Level 5 | Future Scientist  |

Students unlock higher levels by completing lessons.

---

### 3. Interactive STEM Games

#### Mathematics

* Puzzle solving
* Number challenges
* Logic games

#### Science

* Virtual experiments
* Science quizzes

#### Technology

* Coding games
* Computer basics challenges

#### Engineering

* Bridge building simulations
* Design challenges

---

### 4. Fear Reduction Module

Special feature designed to overcome fear of studies.

Includes:

* Small daily challenges
* Confidence-building tasks
* Positive feedback messages
* Progress tracking

Example:
"Great Job! You solved 5 problems today."

---

### 5. Reward System

Students earn:

* Badges
* Achievement certificates
* Virtual trophies
* Coins

Example Badges:

* Math Master
* Science Explorer
* Coding Champion
* STEM Hero

---

### 6. Leaderboard

Friendly competition among:

* Classmates
* School students
* Village students

Focus on participation rather than marks.

---

### 7. Offline Learning Support

Since internet access may be limited:

* Downloadable content
* Offline quizzes
* Low-data mode

---

### 8. STEM Career Stories

Inspirational stories of:

* Scientists
* Engineers
* Innovators

Especially from rural backgrounds.

---

### 9. Teacher Dashboard

Teachers can:

* Track student progress
* Assign challenges
* View reports
* Identify weak areas

---

### 10. Parent Dashboard

Parents can:

* Monitor learning progress
* View achievements
* Encourage daily learning habits

---

## User Journey

1. Student creates account
2. Takes skill assessment
3. Receives personalized STEM path
4. Completes quests and games
5. Earns rewards and badges
6. Advances through levels
